# JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

## Overview
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hirohiko Araki. It began serialization in 1987 and is known for its distinctive art style, creative battles, dramatic poses, supernatural abilities, and memorable characters. The anime adaptation is produced by David Production.

## Story and Structure
The series follows the Joestar family across multiple generations. Each major part features a protagonist whose nickname is "JoJo." Although the stories are connected through family history and recurring themes, each part introduces new characters, settings, conflicts, and abilities.

## Main Story Parts
1. **Phantom Blood** — Jonathan Joestar confronts Dio Brando after Dio enters the Joestar household and acquires a supernatural stone mask.
2. **Battle Tendency** — Joseph Joestar battles ancient beings known as the Pillar Men using strategy, trickery, and Hamon.
3. **Stardust Crusaders** — Jotaro Kujo and his companions travel toward Egypt to confront DIO and save Jotaro's mother.
4. **Diamond Is Unbreakable** — Josuke Higashikata protects the town of Morioh from supernatural threats and a dangerous serial killer.
5. **Golden Wind** — Giorno Giovanna joins a criminal organization in Italy and seeks to change it from within.
6. **Stone Ocean** — Jolyne Cujoh fights for survival in prison while becoming involved in a larger conflict connected to her family's legacy.
7. **Steel Ball Run** — Johnny Joestar and Gyro Zeppeli participate in a cross-country horse race across an alternate version of the United States.
8. **JoJolion** — In the town of Morioh, a young man with amnesia investigates his identity and uncovers a mysterious family history.
9. **The JOJOLands** — Jodio Joestar and his companions pursue wealth in Hawaii while becoming involved in unusual events surrounding valuable objects.

## Important Powers and Concepts

### Hamon
Hamon, also called Ripple, is a technique that uses controlled breathing to produce energy resembling sunlight. It is especially effective against vampires and certain supernatural beings.

### Stands
A Stand is a supernatural manifestation of an individual's fighting spirit or life energy. Stands often possess unique abilities, such as stopping time, manipulating objects, healing, or controlling space. Most Stand abilities have specific conditions and limitations.

### Stone Mask
The Stone Mask is an ancient artifact that can transform humans into vampires under certain circumstances. It plays a central role in the early story.

### Spin
Spin is a technique involving rotational energy. It is particularly important in Steel Ball Run and later storylines, where precise rotation can produce extraordinary effects.

## Major Characters
- **Jonathan Joestar:** The honorable protagonist of Phantom Blood.
- **Joseph Joestar:** A clever and unpredictable fighter featured in Battle Tendency and later parts.
- **Jotaro Kujo:** A central protagonist of Stardust Crusaders, known for his Stand, Star Platinum.
- **Josuke Higashikata:** The protagonist of Diamond Is Unbreakable, whose Stand is Crazy Diamond.
- **Giorno Giovanna:** The protagonist of Golden Wind, whose Stand is Gold Experience.
- **Jolyne Cujoh:** The protagonist of Stone Ocean, whose Stand is Stone Free.
- **Johnny Joestar:** The protagonist of Steel Ball Run, who learns to use the Spin and develops the Stand Tusk.
- **Josuke Higashikata (Part 8):** The amnesiac protagonist of JoJolion, distinct from the Part 4 character with the same name.
- **Jodio Joestar:** The protagonist of The JOJOLands.

## Themes
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure explores family legacy, courage, friendship, ambition, fate, identity, sacrifice, and the conflict between good and evil. Its battles often emphasize creativity and tactical reasoning rather than raw strength alone.

## Interesting Facts
- The series is famous for dramatic character poses and highly stylized fashion.
- Many character and Stand names reference musicians, songs, albums, or bands.
- The story shifts between historical periods, countries, and generations.
- Stand battles frequently reward careful observation, experimentation, and clever use of unusual abilities.

## Summary
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is a long-running supernatural action series that combines generational storytelling, distinctive characters, unusual powers, strategic combat, and dramatic visual presentation. Its defining features are the Joestar family legacy, Hamon, Stands, and imaginative battles.
